Food delivery services, such as Zomato and Swiggy, are essential in urban life, processing millions of orders daily. A key factor in customer satisfaction is accurate delivery time estimates; delays can lead to negative reviews. External conditions like traffic, weather, and festivals significantly impact delivery times. The project analyzes a dataset with 45,593 records from Indian cities to identify influential factors and build a predictive model in R Studio to accurately estimate delivery duration, covering the complete data science workflow, including data cleaning and machine learning evaluation.
